## Frequently asked questions

### The call won't connect or the AI doesn't respond
Check that you have a working internet connection and that you've granted
microphone access (**iOS Settings → Frontier → Microphone**). Fully close the
app and reopen it, then try again.

### How do I change the voice or the AI model?
Pick a voice from the Rolodex on the home screen before starting a call. The
AI model and other options live in **Settings**.

### Is my conversation private?
Your conversation transcripts are stored **only on your device** — we don't
keep them on our servers. Audio is streamed only during an active call and is
not recorded or saved by us. See our
[Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy' | relative_url }}) for full detail.

### How do I delete my data?
Deleting the app removes all locally stored conversations and settings. You can
also use **Delete account** inside the app to wipe local data. Because we hold
no personal data on our servers, there is nothing further to delete.

### I was charged / I want a refund
Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. Refund requests are managed
through Apple at **[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)**.
